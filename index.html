On-chain collection viewer + transfer flow

How it runs

Four steps,
in order

No discovery theater. You see something real on a screen within days, and every stage after that revises a working thing instead of a document about one. Timings below are for a full site; a single landing page compresses to under a week.

01

Scope

Day 1 — $750, credited

A paid working session where we pin down what the site has to do, who it talks to, and what counts as success. You leave with a written scope and a fixed price, and the $750 comes off the project total if we go ahead. If we are not a fit, we find out on day one — not one quarter in.

02

Direction

Days 2–4

One direction, built in the browser at full fidelity — not three static comps to pick between. You react to the real thing on your real device, usually before the first week is out. Two rounds of revision are built into this stage.

03

Build

Week 2

The full site or product on a staging URL you can check any day of the week. Content, responsive behavior, accessibility, a performance budget, and tests on whatever logic is load-bearing.

04

Launch

Week 3, then 30 days

Deploy, analytics, and a handoff walkthrough recorded so you can rewatch it, plus thirty days of fixes included. After that you either run it yourself or keep me on retainer — your call, no default.
